How the simulation works

  • Each trade puts your chosen share of the bankroll at that moment on the line, so wins and losses compound trade by trade.
  • P/L is a percentage of the position. A trade that loses −100% wipes out the money in that trade, not the whole account.
  • Trades win at random at your win rate. Each win pays the average win and each loss costs the average loss.
  • The chart runs 1,000 simulated careers from the same inputs. The bold line is the median run, and the shaded fan holds the middle 50% and 80% of runs.
  • Market holidays follow the NYSE schedule. Fees, slippage, taxes, deposits and withdrawals are left out.
